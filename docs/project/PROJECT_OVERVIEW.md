# Project Overview

## Product Identity & Goals
**SolarFlow Manager** is a multi-tenant, offline-first solar water pump sizing, management, sales, and financial ecosystem. It is specifically designed to support the business workflows and field operations of **Meseret Mare Solar** and **Fasil Zelalem (FZ) Trading**.

The primary goals are:
- **Accuracy in Sizing:** Connect the field operator directly to AI-driven calculations using NASA solar/insolation databases and official manufacturer specs.
- **Financial Integrity:** Build a secure ledger with dual-entry reporting to minimize payment delays and track budget approvals.
- **Offline Readiness:** Support operators in remote locations with bad cellular coverage by syncing records client-to-server.
- **Structured Hierarchy:** Ensure sequential routing of tasks and financial requests according to corporate structure.
